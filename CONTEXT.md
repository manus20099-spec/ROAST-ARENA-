# Startup Roast Arena

A student platform where founders post startup ideas, three AI investors roast them and offer fake funding, and the community votes. A leaderboard ranks the best ideas.

## Language

**Founder**:
A registered user who posts an Idea.
_Avoid_: Author, creator, poster

**Idea**:
A startup concept owned by one Founder. It persists across re-pitches and collects the community's votes.
_Avoid_: Post, project, startup

**Pitch**:
One versioned submission of an Idea: the problem, the solution, the customer and a roast level. Re-pitching adds a new Pitch to the same Idea.
_Avoid_: Version, submission, draft

**Roast Level**:
The tone the investors use on a Pitch: Mild, Medium or Spicy. It changes tone only, never scoring or offer rules.
_Avoid_: Spice level, difficulty

**Investor**:
One of three AI personalities (Strict, Funny, Dreamer) that reviews a Pitch.
_Avoid_: Judge, bot, reviewer

**Roast**:
One Investor's response to a Pitch: a score, a short roast, one improvement tip and a funding offer.
_Avoid_: Review, feedback, comment

**Re-pitch**:
The act of a Founder submitting a new Pitch to an existing Idea after feedback. A Pitch's text is never edited in place; every change is a re-pitch.
_Avoid_: Edit, update, resubmit

**Pending Pitch**:
A Pitch still waiting for all three Roasts. It cannot be ranked or voted on.
_Avoid_: In-progress pitch, processing pitch

**Complete Pitch**:
A Pitch that has received all three Roasts, whether written by the AI or by fallback. Only an Idea with a Complete Pitch can be ranked or voted on.
_Avoid_: Finished pitch, scored pitch

**Flagged Pitch**:
A Pitch the backend judged suspicious (for example, unanimous top scores on near-empty text), kept out of the Leaderboard.
_Avoid_: Rejected pitch, banned pitch

**Ranked Pitch**:
The Idea's newest Complete Pitch that is not Flagged. The Leaderboard reads only this Pitch's scores. An Idea with no Ranked Pitch is not on the Leaderboard.
_Avoid_: Current pitch, active pitch, latest pitch

**Fallback Roast**:
A Roast written by the backend's template rules instead of an Investor's AI, used when the AI fails repeatedly. It is always labelled as such.
_Avoid_: Fake roast, default roast, offline roast

**Offer**:
The fake funding an Investor proposes in a Roast, such as an amount for a stake of equity.
_Avoid_: Bid, deal

**Vote**:
A user's single upvote on an Idea. Removable, never given on one's own Idea. It counts toward the Leaderboard only once the voter's account is an hour old.
_Avoid_: Like, upvote (as a noun), rating

**Leaderboard**:
The ranking of Ideas by a mix of the Ranked Pitch's AI scores and the Idea's eligible Votes.
_Avoid_: Rankings, top list
